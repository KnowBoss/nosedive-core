# Base

A plain seed makes a base root in kb/. A hero primary root is of kind hero in self/ when newly seeded; any further root it lists may be a base, optional, holding what the hero knows about clients, people or domains. Existing configured directories stay where they are. Each root can wear capes through its cape-role links; selection behavior is unchanged.

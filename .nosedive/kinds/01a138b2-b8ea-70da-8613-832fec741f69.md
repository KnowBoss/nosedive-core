# Memo

The one open kind. A memo becomes a root, a feat or a grouping by how it is linked, not by its kind. It is not durable: a memo is work on the way to something kept, and what it settles moves into durable docs, such as actions, functions and decisions, before it is dropped.

## Done when

What it was written for is done, reviewed and tested, and whatever it settled lives on in durable docs.

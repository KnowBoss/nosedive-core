# Ship

The kind of an older repo's root: before a repo was what its config enables, a ship was the repo pilots worked from, its root of this kind carrying `meta.workspace`. Such a root is still read that way: its repo's workspace is its `meta.workspace`, `./workspace` unless it says otherwise. Seed makes no new ones; a repo does work by having `workspace` in its config.

The workspace is where the repos it works in are checked out. Seed makes the folder, with a `.gitignore` of `*` inside, so nothing checked out there is committed and the repo's own `.gitignore` is left alone; when the path changes, the next seed moves the workspace. Writing a doc into a repo checked out there, with `crud <repo>:<kind>`, refuses anywhere else.

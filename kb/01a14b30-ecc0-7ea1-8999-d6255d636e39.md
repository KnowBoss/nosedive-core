# Crud edit

`key` is the edit's flag without its dashes, and `value` what the flag takes, inline: text for `title`, `body`, `gist`, `name` and `kind`, a quid for `parent`, a mapping for `meta`, `scopes` and `links`, `{heading, text}` for `section` and `{old, new}` for `replace`. Each refuses as its flag would. `--batch` applies each item through it, and a gate's `crud` is it, bound to the pass's edit.

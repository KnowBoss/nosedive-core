# Render tree

Reads the root's links: each rel ending `.feat`, other than `parent.feat`, names a group, rendered as a `## ` section with the group's first letter raised, groups in alphabetical order. Each doc in a group is a line, `- [<title>](<id>.md): <gist>`, the title being its `# ` heading or else its name, sorted by title; its `child.feat` docs follow, indented two more spaces, to any depth. Links may be written `kb/<id>.md` or as a bare id; a link to a doc the kb does not hold, or one that would revisit a doc already on the path, is left out. Kinds do not matter.

The result is the root's whole body, its title first.

# Read repo config

Unlike find-bridge, it never looks above the directory, so a repo checked out in a ship's workspace reads as itself. Nothing is there when the directory has neither a `.nosedive/config.yaml` nor a kind file.

A repo's kind file is the one of `.nosedive/ship.md`, `cape.md` and `base.md` it holds; holding two is an error. Its id is the repo's root and identity. A ship's decks and workspace are its `ship.md`'s meta, its kb is its first deck, and its kinds and repos are kept in `.nosedive/kinds/` and `.nosedive/repos/`; a cape's or base's docs are in its config's `kb`, else `./kb`.

Without a kind file, the root is whichever of `ship`, `cape`, `base`, `root` and `backlog` the config sets, two of them naming different docs being an error, and the kb is its config's `kb`. Such a repo is a ship when its root is a `ship`, or a legacy `base` carrying `meta.workspace`, and has one deck, its kb.

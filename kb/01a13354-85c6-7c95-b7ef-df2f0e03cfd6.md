# Find bridge

The bridge is the repo whose config nosedive resolves when it runs: the nearest hero above the directory, unless that hero is itself checked out in another hero's workspace, in which case that other hero. A hero repo here is a repo with a workspace. With no hero above, it is the nearest repo nosedive knows, by its `.nosedive/config.yaml`. Each repo is read with read-repo-config: its id, its roots and kb, and its workspace.

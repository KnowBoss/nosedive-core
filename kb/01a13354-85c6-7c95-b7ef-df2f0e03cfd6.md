# Find bridge

The bridge is the repo whose config nosedive resolves when it runs: the nearest ship above the directory, unless that ship is itself checked out in another ship's workspace, in which case that other ship. With no ship above, it is the nearest repo with a `.nosedive/config.yaml`. Each repo's own config is read with read-repo-config: its kb, its root, and, for a ship, its workspace.

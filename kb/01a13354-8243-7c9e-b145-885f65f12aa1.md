# Match usage

Takes docopt-style usages and an argv (action name first). Options may come in any order, as `--opt value` or `--opt=value`; `-` alone is a value, not an option. Bound names are camelCased with brackets and dashes stripped: `<gist>...` binds `gist` to a list, `--name <name>` binds `name`, a bare `--flag` binds `flag` to true. No match, or more than one, throws with the usages listed.

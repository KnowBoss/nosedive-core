# Deck

A ship's docs are kept in decks, each a directory with a root of this kind; the root's links are the deck's tree, and feats hang in decks, never in bases or capes. A ship lists its decks in its `ship.md`, as `meta.decks: { <dir>: <root quid> }`, and seed makes the first, `./kb`, with its root named after the repo. Helm's deck picker shows a deck by its root's title.

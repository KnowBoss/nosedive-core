# Root

What a repo's docs hang from when nothing more is said: seed mints one, `kb/<quid>.md`, named after the repo, and lists it in config `roots`. A root's links are its tree, which seed renders as its body. Feats it reaches are what will be built: worked through dives where the repo has a workspace, and otherwise kept, as a table keeps rows. Any kind may be a root; a root of kind `cape` makes its repo a cape instead.

The schema is closed and declares nothing; it is durable, so feats may hang off it.

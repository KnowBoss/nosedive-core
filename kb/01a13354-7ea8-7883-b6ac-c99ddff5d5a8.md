# Seed

On a repo with no `.nosedive/config.yaml`, seed mints the bridge's id, writes the config (`bridge`, `kb: ./kb`), creates the kb, ignores `.nosedive/capes/`, and commits. On a bridge, it adds whatever of that is missing and leaves everything else alone.

Building the capes the config's `actions` name is not in this prototype yet; seed says so when there are any.

# Doc kind

A doc's kind is its owner and its name, and every reader in core that matches a doc's `kind` for what it means goes through this function: describe-cape-source, scaf, find-root, pass, drop and check-meta. The owner is a repo's seeded id, the `id` in its `.nosedive/config.yaml`; core's is `01a1336b-c0b8-7cde-b7c6-285196062297`.

- A doc in `.nosedive/kinds/` is core's `kind`, and one in `.nosedive/repos/` core's `repo`, by place, whatever its `kind` says.
- Otherwise a doc with a scope entry marked `canonical: true` has the kind named by its root `kind`, owned by that entry's key. Two canonical entries are refused.
- Otherwise its root `kind` is its holder's own kind. The holder is the seeded repo whose git checkout holds the doc, found by walking up from its path unless given: the hero for its own docs, a checkout's own repo for a doc in a seeded workspace repo. A repo that is not seeded holds nothing, though it sits in a hero's workspace, so such a doc has no owner.

`scopeEntries` is the one reading of a doc's scopes, a list of one-key mappings. A key repeated is refused. An entry carries `canonical`, `version`, `schema` (a digest of the kind's schema at that version, see find-kind) and `meta`. Work scopes, keyed by a repo doc's quid, are entries like any other.
